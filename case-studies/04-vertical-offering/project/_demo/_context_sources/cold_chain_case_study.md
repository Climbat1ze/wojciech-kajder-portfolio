# Cold Chain Logistics Deployment — Case Study (excerpt, fictional)

A regional cold-storage distributor equipped its pickers with the Aster
Field Tablet for a winter pilot. Devices operated without failure across a
temperature range of -20C to 45C between the freezer aisles and the loading
dock. The 900-nit display stayed readable in direct sunlight at the outdoor
dock, and the hot-swappable 8500mAh battery let workers swap power mid-shift
without restarting the device or losing their place in the current pick
list. The pilot also surfaced a durability question the distributor asked
about directly: the vendor's drop-rating claim (1.5m to concrete, targeting
MIL-STD-810H) had not yet been independently confirmed at the time of this
report.
