# Executive summary

Phase 1 is on track for the 12 October go-live, but the date depends on one thing outside our control being fixed now: the client's legacy data cleanup.

Scope, acceptance criteria, and the cutover plan are all agreed and documented. The delivery team is executing to plan and reporting honestly. Two things need leadership attention. First, the legacy data quality issue has no owner on the client side and directly threatens the migration; it is the single biggest risk to the date. Second, the client's request to move the loyalty module into phase 1 would stretch a team that is already at full capacity, and would need either an extra developer or a later date.

The recommendation into the next steering committee is to hold the 12 October date, secure a named client owner and deadline for the data cleanup this week, and decide the loyalty change explicitly rather than letting it absorb slack by default.
