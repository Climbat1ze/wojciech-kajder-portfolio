## Delivery shape

- Phase 1 scope baseline: store inventory, point-of-sale integration, customer profile service.
- Single go-live target of 12 October, with a migration dry-run the week before and a cutover over 10-11 October.
- Acceptance is gated on three measurable criteria agreed up front.

## Escalation path

Open items that cannot be resolved in working correspondence go to the steering committee. Scope changes against the baseline are handled through change control, owned by the sponsor.
