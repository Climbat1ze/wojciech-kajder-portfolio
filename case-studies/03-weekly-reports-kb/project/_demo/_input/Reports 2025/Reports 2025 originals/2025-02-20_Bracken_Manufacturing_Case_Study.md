Title: Bracken Manufacturing Shop-Floor Rollout — Case Study
Author: Elena Rossi
Created: 2025-02-20

Case Study
----------
Bracken Manufacturing Co. piloted Meridian Access Platform on 400 shop-floor
tablets at its Denver plant during a six-week evaluation completed in Q1 2025;
device provisioning time dropped from 42 minutes to 6 minutes per unit,
attributed to the bulk certificate renewal feature shipped in Device
Enrollment Service v1.4. These results directly informed the company-wide
3,800-unit rollout proposal that remained under review as of late February
2025.
